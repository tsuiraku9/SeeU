# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
personal attacks, doxxing, and publication of another person's credentials or
private data are not acceptable.

Assume good faith while reviewing ideas, but evaluate technical and safety
claims on evidence. Keep criticism focused on the work. Maintainers may edit,
hide, or remove abusive or unsafe contributions and may restrict participation
when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/tsuiraku9). Do not publish
personal or sensitive details in an issue.
